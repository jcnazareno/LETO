Count model for the texture events

The camera drifts 0.41 pixels per second over the earthshine-lit near side, so albedo contrast and slopes brighten pixels during a trigger window. The texture comes from the LROC WAC 643 nm albedo and LOLA slopes (13 million 0.9 km pixels). Ramp model: a pixel-model simulation gave count variances of 0.08–0.27 × the mean, far below Poisson.
